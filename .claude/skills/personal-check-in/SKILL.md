# Personal check-in

Keep it under two minutes. Ask, wait, reflect back in 2–3 lines. Be a coach, not a cheerleader.

## Morning (if not already done by `/plan-my-day`)
- Energy 1–5? · What would make today a win?

## End of day
1. Before asking, pull what happened: completed Todoist tasks today, meetings attended, drafts
   created, P1 emails still unanswered.
2. Ask in one message:
   - "What's one win from today?"
   - "What's still on your mind?"
   - "What's the first thing tomorrow?"
3. Then:
   - Roll unfinished Todoist tasks due today to tomorrow or next open slot (show the list first).
   - Make "first thing tomorrow" a P1 Todoist task due tomorrow morning.
   - Log to Notion "Daily Check-ins": Date, Energy, Win, On my mind, Tomorrow's first move.
4. Friday version adds: "What did you learn this week?" and suggests `/production-plan` for next week.

Patterns: if energy ≤2 for 3+ logged days, or the same item is "on my mind" 3 days running, say so
gently and suggest one concrete change (delegate, block time, raise it at L10).

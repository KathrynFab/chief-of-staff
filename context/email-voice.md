# Email voice

How Kathryn writes. `/draft-email` follows this. Paste 2–3 real sent emails below (with client
details removed) to make drafts sound more like you.

- Warm, direct, and short. Lead with the ask or the decision in the first line.
- Use first names. Greeting: "Hi <name>," — sign-off: "Thanks,\nKathryn"
- Bullets for more than two items; bold the due date when there is one.
- Recognize the person's work before redirecting it.
- Never include client names or health details in emails to anyone outside the care team;
  refer to the client by initials or "the client we discussed" when in doubt.

## Role at a glance
- Reports to: **Joh Olson, President**. Level: executive leadership. Full-time, exempt.
- Scope: all five ApexCare offices — Sacramento, Stockton, Vacaville, Concord, Napa.
- Leads directly: branch managers (one per office), Scheduling Manager, Lead Care Partner team.
- Leads through branch managers: Client Success, Care Partner Success (CPSM) and care coordination teams.

## Position summary
Build, protect and optimize ApexCare's operations so the company scales without service or culture
roadblocks: build self-sufficient office teams led by branch managers who own their numbers, then
support and oversee them rather than run every task personally. Mission: be the **Brightest Light**.
Values: Excellence, Heart, Accountability, Unreasonable Hospitality, Build jobs worth keeping.

**Three priorities come first:** (1) build a strong team, (2) run a smart budget, (3) recruit well and
keep Care Partners.

## Areas of responsibility
1. **Build the team** — right branch manager in every office; clear JD + KPIs for every role; coach
   branch managers to run tight L10s; 1:1s, evaluations, accountability with candor and care;
   leadership pipeline (Scheduling Manager, Lead Care Partner team, successors).
2. **Protect the culture & Care Partners** — core values in daily language then action; monthly
   in-person time with each office + short 1:1s; employee surveys acted on; Care Partners treated as
   team members starting with onboarding; company-wide training program run by CPSMs.
3. **Deliver consistent client service** — consistent standards across offices; fast resolution of
   client concerns/escalations; reliable management + CRM on-call, backup and escalation; good use of
   WellSky and approved systems.
4. **Optimize & grow** — re-evaluate processes and tighten accountability; own a realistic operations
   budget; get offices ready for new clients, programs and acquisitions (incl. CalAIM); accurate, timely
   reporting to executive leadership; support Mimi & Grace and ECM integration.
5. **Hand-offs** — keep accountability, move day-to-day work to a person or system, check weekly
   (see table below).

## Scorecard
About 15 results across four areas; targets set with the President each quarter.

| Area | What we track | Reviewed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Branch leadership | Each office has a branch manager who owns their KPIs · weekly L10 in every office · share of office scorecard measures on track · weekly billable hours by office | Weekly (L10); quarterly with the President |
| Care Partners | CP churn incl. first-100-day churn · share of new hires at training in first month · hours wanted vs. scheduled · CP reviews and recognition | Monthly; reviews every 6 months |
| Clients | Satisfaction score vs. standard · client churn · clients whose hours increased · clients who return | Monthly |
| Growth | Offices added/acquired · hours, clients and CPs per office from acquisition · employee satisfaction at and after acquisition | Quarterly |

Context: Care Partner turnover ≈78% company-wide in 2025; target <50% by 2029.

## Decision rights
- **VP decides:** management team supervision/accountability; operational hiring and approved
  separations; client escalations and service delivery; performance standards; CP training, field ops
  and resource allocation; management + CRM on-call; rollout of approved initiatives.
- **Take to the President:** significant financial commitments, budget changes, strategy changes,
  anything outside operational authority.

## Recurring cadence
- Weekly: ApexCare L10; office L10s (rollout); 1:1s with branch managers, Scheduling Manager, Lead CPs;
  weekly check on hand-offs (on-call, fleet, supplies).
- Monthly: in-person time with each office team; CP and client scorecard review.
- Quarterly: targets with the President; client visits and work a shift; QBR/Rocks.

## Purpose
Run ApexCare's day-to-day home care operation across 5 offices and 8 NorCal counties so that
every scheduled visit is covered by a well-matched care partner, clients stay longer, offices hit
their numbers, and the company is always inspection-ready.

## Areas of responsibility
1. **Office performance** — lead office/branch leaders; billable hours, census, gross margin and
   visit fill rate per office; weekly review of the office Scorecard.
2. **Staffing & scheduling** — coverage, fill rate, call-outs, overtime, scheduling efficiency
   (3-hour minimums), care-partner recruiting pipeline with HR.
3. **Care-partner retention** — onboarding, training, recognition, turnover by office, the retention plan.
4. **Client experience** — intake-to-start speed, care quality, complaints and escalations,
   client tenure and churn.
5. **Compliance & quality** — CDSS Home Care Services Bureau licensing, Home Care Aide Registry,
   training hours, incident reporting, audit readiness, policies & procedures.
6. **EOS & leadership rhythm** — ApexCare L10; Rock: bring an L10 to every office; office-level
   Scorecards, issues lists and to-dos.
7. **Growth partnership** — support GTM/referral partners on service capacity and pricing;
   expansion readiness for new territories.
8. **Systems & process** — WellSky, HomeCare HQ and BI/Metabase adoption; feedback to product;
   standard work across offices.
9. **Budget & labor cost** — office budgets, labor cost %, overtime control, vendor spend.

## Scorecard (weekly KPIs to watch)
| KPI | Owner area | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Billable hours (by office) | Office performance | _set_ |
| Visit fill rate / uncovered shifts | Staffing | _set_ |
| New client starts / lost clients | Client experience | _set_ |
| Care-partner hires / terms (turnover) | Retention | _set_ |
| Overtime % of hours | Budget | _set_ |
| Open incidents / complaints | Compliance & quality | _set_ |
| Offices running an L10 | EOS | 5 of 5 |

## Recurring cadence
- Weekly: ApexCare L10 (attend), office L10s (roll out / attend), 1:1s with direct reports,
  Scorecard review.
- Monthly: office performance review, compliance check (training, registry, incidents).
- Quarterly: QBR and Rock setting.

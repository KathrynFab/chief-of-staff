# Working preferences

Edit freely — every skill reads this file.

## Day shape (Pacific)
- Workday: 8:00–5:30
- Protect: 8:00–9:00 for plan + inbox; one 90-minute focus block per day
- No meetings before: 8:30
- Lunch: 12:00–12:30
- Shutdown: 5:00 end-of-day check-in

## Inbox
- Triage twice a day: morning (with `/plan-my-day`) and ~2pm.
- VIP senders (always P1/P2): leadership team (MS, MH, JO, JW), direct reports, CDSS/HCSB,
  anything with "call-out", "uncovered", "incident", "complaint", "fall", "urgent".
- Low priority: newsletters, vendor outreach, automated notifications.

## Todoist
- Production plan project: "Production Plan" (sections = JD areas)
- Labels: @waiting, @delegated, @L10, @1on1-<name>, @deep-work, @quick
- Priority mapping: P1 = safety/coverage or due today, P2 = commitment this week, P3 = plan work, P4 = someday

## Check-ins
- Personal check-in: morning (2 questions) and end of day (3 questions). Keep it under 2 minutes.
